About Me

I am a game designer and gameplay programmer with a B.S. in Game Design and Interactive Media from the University of California, Irvine. My experience spans Unity, Godot, and multiplayer game development, with a focus on gameplay systems, AI architecture, and networked experiences. I enjoy building scalable systems that allow designers to iterate quickly while maintaining strong technical foundations. My work includes behavior tree AI, finite state machines, multiplayer networking, resource management systems, and gameplay architecture.

Languages: C#, GDScript, C++

Engines & Tools: Unity, Godot, Unreal Engine 5, Photon PUN / Fusion, Git

Specialties: Multiplayer Networking, AI (FSMs & Behavior Trees), Gameplay Systems, Physics Programming, Optimization, Rapid Prototyping
